# Capacity Desk privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-09

Capacity Desk keeps everything you enter on your own device. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. The developer never receives your data.

## What the app stores

Capacity Desk stores only what you type into it, in a database file inside the app's private storage on your device:

- Your weekly schedule: classes, commutes and other fixed commitments, with their names, days and times.
- Work shifts: dates, times and any note you add.
- Training: your weekly sessions, their names, type, intensity and length, and whether each one was done, moved or skipped.
- Study and project blocks: date, time, length, label, the course they belong to, whether they were done, and the energy rating (1 to 5) you gave them.
- Daily check-ins: your energy rating (1 to 5) and, if you choose to enter it, the number of hours you slept.
- Courses and grades: course names, credits, target grades, and each assessment's name, type, weight, due date, score and target study hours.
- Settings: your sleep and wake times, daily overhead, training minimum, project hours, notification choices and any tuning values you changed.

The app also remembers the last course and block length you logged, to pre-fill the logging screen.

## What the app does not do

- It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- It does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else. The app makes no network requests.
- It does not include advertising, analytics, crash reporting or any third-party tracking code.
- It does not read your contacts, calendar, location, camera, microphone, files, health sensors or other apps.

## Notifications

Notifications are off until you turn them on in Settings. If you turn them on, Android asks for permission to show notifications.

Reminders are created on your device from your own schedule. They are not sent from a server.

## Android permissions

The app declares these permissions, and no others:

- Show notifications: asked for only when you turn a notification on.
- Run at startup: so Android can restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
- Prevent the device from sleeping: used for a moment when a reminder is delivered.
- Network access: declared by the standard component that displays the app. Capacity Desk does not use it; the app loads only files that are inside it.

## Export and import

You can export your data as a JSON backup or as CSV files from Settings. Export opens your device's share sheet and you choose where the file goes. Once a file leaves the app, it is covered by the privacy practices of wherever you send it.

Importing a backup replaces the data in the app with the contents of the file you pick. The file is read on your device only.

## Device backup

The app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so its database is not copied to your Google account. To move your data to a new phone, export a backup and import it there.

## Deleting your data

Settings has an option that erases everything in the app. Uninstalling the app also deletes its database from your device. Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing to request or delete elsewhere.

## Children

Capacity Desk is intended for university students and adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app stores or starts sending any data off your device, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
